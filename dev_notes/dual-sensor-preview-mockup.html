What this mockup is claiming

§7.6 of the design doc, in the smallest number of words
  • The picker always ships. Even with the inset on, something has to say which sensor is the main pane — so the picker is the substrate, not the alternative. On a single-sensor camera its two <option>s are left exactly as authored and no DOM write happens at all.
  • The inset is deliberately less than the main pane. Other sensor, sub stream, buffered MSE only, muted, no watchdog, no WHEP, and it shares no variable with the main player — so it cannot wedge it.
  • Cost, from src/config.c:341-376: +512 kb/s on a 3000 kb/s main pane (+17 % uplink) and 640×360×15 = 3.46 Mpx/s on top of 31.1 Mpx/s (+11 % decode). The cost that is not negligible is the third long-lived connection to port 8880 — see preview.html:795-805, a measured hang — which is why the inset is default-off and torn down whenever the tab is hidden.
  • And it costs the camera, not just the client. timps encodes on demand: hal_ingenic.c:2091 gates IMP_Encoder_StartRecvPic on vc->active || hub_active(vc->si), and :440-445 records that leaving a framesource enabled with no clients was ~19 % idle CPU. Opening the inset wakes sensor 1's substream framesource and encoder that were otherwise stopped — on a T23 whose dual-sensor headroom §9.1 says nobody has measured.
  • Drag then corner-snap, not free positioning: a corner survives a resize, and it is the least likely place to want the PTZ joystick that lives under this same surface.
  • Click the inset to swap. It is its own affordance.
